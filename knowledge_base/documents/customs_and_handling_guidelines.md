# Customs Clearance & Special Material Handling Standards (SOP-CUS-003)

## 1. Customs Procedures & Delay Mitigation
- **Standard Clearance**: Average clearance duration is 1.5 to 2.5 days.
- **FastTrack Priority**: Authorized for medical, semiconductor, and critical automotive assemblies. Clearance under 24 hours.
- **Inspection Hold**: Subject to mandatory physical customs inspection. Historically correlates with an additional 5.0 to 12.0 days of port dwell delay. All shipments entering Inspection Hold must be flagged to the Inventory Risk Agent.
- **Bonded Transit**: Sealed bonded transport between designated free-trade zones.

## 2. Material Handling & Special Transit Controls
- **Cold Chain Refrigerated**: Strict temperature compliance (+2C to +8C). Shelf-life sensitive compounds such as polymer electrolyte membranes and conductive pastes require expedited transit and real-time data loggers.
- **Hazardous HazMat**: Regulated chemical fluids and high-density lithium-ion battery modules (e.g., PROD-002, PROD-027). Requires certified dangerous goods carriers and specialized customs manifests.
- **Fragile Sensitive**: Optical sensors and silicon microcontrollers requiring antistatic packaging and vibration isolation.

# Logistics Disruption & Route Incident Playbook (SOP-LOG-004)

## 1. Port Congestion and Carrier Bottlenecks
When primary ocean/intermodal routes (such as Shanghai to Los Angeles or Shenzhen to Chicago) experience bottleneck delays exceeding 5 days:
1. Re-route high-priority and critical inventory shipments to air freight or expedited multimodal hubs (e.g., Hub-East or Hub-Central).
2. Contact 3PL carriers (DHL Global, FedEx SupplyChain, Maersk Logistics) to verify customs transit clearance status.
3. Review active purchase orders with transit distances exceeding 10,000 km for possible split shipments.

## 2. Lead Time Variance Management
Unusual lead-time increases greater than 2.5 standard deviations from the historical 90-day moving average must be flagged as operational anomalies. The Delivery Risk Agent and Logistics Controller must review carrier route allocations and evaluate secondary logistics corridors.

## 3. Expedited Freight Authorization Criteria
Expedited air freight is pre-authorized if:
- Affected product current inventory is below 7 days of supply (DOS <= 7.0).
- Downstream assembly stoppage penalty exceeds air freight surcharge.

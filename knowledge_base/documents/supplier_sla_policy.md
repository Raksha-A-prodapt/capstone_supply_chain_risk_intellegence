# Global Supplier SLA & Performance Policy (SOP-SUP-001)

## 1. On-Time Delivery Service Level Agreements
Suppliers contracted under Tier 1 and Tier 2 agreements must maintain a minimum On-Time In-Full (OTIF) delivery rate of 88.0%. 
A delivery is formally classified as a `late_order` if the actual delivery timestamp exceeds the agreed `expected_delivery_date` by more than 24 hours.

## 2. Supplier Risk Thresholds & Escalation
- **Low Risk (< 0.25)**: Supplier meets or exceeds 92% on-time performance with lead-time standard deviation under 2.0 days.
- **Medium Risk (0.25 - 0.50)**: Late rate between 12% and 25%. Triggers bi-weekly operational review and buffer stock adjustments.
- **High Risk (0.50 - 0.75)**: Late rate between 25% and 50%, or recurring delays exceeding 5 days. Triggers dual-sourcing contingency and procurement audits.
- **Critical Risk (>= 0.75)**: Late rate exceeding 50% or severe lead-time anomalies exceeding 3.0 standard deviations (z-score > 3.0). Triggers executive hold, contract freeze, and immediate reallocation of orders to secondary suppliers.

## 3. Disruption Penalties and Liquidated Damages
For delays exceeding 7 consecutive business days not attributed to Force Majeure, suppliers will be assessed liquidated damages of 1.5% of order value per delayed business day, capped at 15% of the total purchase order.
